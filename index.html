How to Play

Guess the secret smartphone. Each guess shows color-coded feedback for 7 specs.

✓

Green — Exact match. For numeric fields: within a very tight range.

~

Amber — Close, but not exact. Screen within ±0.4", charging within ±22W, camera within ±18MP.

✗

Gray — Far off or completely wrong.

↕

Arrows on numeric tiles tell you if the real value is higher ↑ or lower ↓.

💡

A random spec hint is shown at the top — use it!

Scoring: 1→1000 pts · 2→800 · 3→600 · 4→400 · 5→200 · 6+→100
